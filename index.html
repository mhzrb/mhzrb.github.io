# 01 — about

About

I'm a Software Engineer with nearly six years of professional experience across software development and technical education, with the past several years focused hands on on building backend systems, APIs, automation tools, and data driven applications.

My core experience is in Python and Django, along with REST APIs, SQL databases, Linux, Docker, and cloud environments. I've also worked with React and TypeScript, and I have a broader technical foundation in C/C++ and Java.

I'm particularly interested in backend, systems, cloud, and platform engineering. I enjoy solving problems around reliability, automation, performance, integrations, and scalable software design, and I care about building software that is clean, maintainable, and dependable.

I like understanding how systems behave behind the scenes, making thoughtful technical decisions, and continuously improving the way I build software. I'm especially interested in distributed systems, software architecture, and modern infrastructure technologies.

I'm currently based in the Netherlands and authorized to work here without a TWV. I'm always happy to connect with developers, engineers, and technology professionals, and I'm open to software engineering opportunities where I can contribute, learn, and work on technically meaningful products.

Outside of software, I'm an avid reader across many fields, from technical books to fiction and everything in between.
